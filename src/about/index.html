About Oluwatosin Alli

Senior Full-stack Engineer, Computer Scientist, and CEO, building in Abuja, Nigeria.

Oluwatosin Alli is a Senior Full-stack Engineer and Computer Scientist based in Abuja, Nigeria. He is CEO of Hustlrs Nigeria and Nadiron Technologies, and holds a B.Sc. in Computer Science with an AI Specialization from Babcock University.

What I do

Nine years of full-stack engineering across fintech, healthcare, education, and social platforms: architecting scalable systems, shipping production applications, and leading engineering teams as a CTO before founding my own companies. My work spans backend systems in Node.js, TypeScript, and .NET Core, frontend applications in React and Next.js, and blockchain systems in Solidity and Web3.js.

Companies

I'm CEO of Hustlrs Nigeria, a task-auction marketplace built for how Nigerians actually hustle, and Nadiron Technologies, the studio behind products including Estelle, an AI agent that helps shy, high-skill people build a personal brand.

Education & credentials

B.Sc. Computer Science (AI Specialization), Babcock University, September 2020 to July 2024. Certified as an AWS Certified Solutions Architect, MongoDB Certified Developer, Google Cloud Professional Developer, and Certified Scrum Master.

Technical skills

Backend: Node.js, TypeScript, .NET Core, Python, Java (Spring Boot), funC, Nest.js
Frontend: JavaScript, React, React Native, Next.js, Vue.js, HTML5, CSS3
Database: MongoDB, PostgreSQL, MySQL, Redis
DevOps: Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, AWS, Google Cloud
Blockchain: Solidity, Web3.js, Ethers.js, TON Blockchain

Faith

I'm a Christian, and it isn't separate from how I work. Habits like weekly Sabbath rest, prayer before production deploys, and daily Scripture reading show up directly in how I lead my companies and approach engineering. I write about the specifics on the blog rather than just asserting it here.

Where I'm based

Abuja, Nigeria, after nine years of remote freelance and CTO work for companies from Miami to Alabama. I build from here deliberately, not by default.
