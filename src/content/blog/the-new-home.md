There is a specific kind of silence that comes with starting over.

For the past nine years, I've built, scaled, and shipped for others. I've worn the hats of a CTO, a lead developer, and a founder. I've lived in the codebases of companies from Miami to Alabama, and now, back home in Abuja. But today feels different. Today, I'm finally carving out a digital space that belongs entirely to me.

**tosinxt.com** is more than just a URL. It's a milestone.

It's solemn to look back at the journey—from studying computer science at Babcock to the late nights spent building systems like Bitcash or Estelle. There's a weight to the experience you carry after nearly a decade in full-stack engineering. You realize that while the tech changes, the purpose remains: we build to bridge gaps.

But beneath that reflection is a restless excitement.

I'm currently setting up the final pieces of this infrastructure. From the simple act of creating `hello@tosinxt.com` to refining my portfolio, every step feels like a fresh coat of paint on a new home. This site won't just be a resume; it's going to be a laboratory for my ideas—from Web3 and P2P platforms to my experiments with music and "Duolingo for couples."

I've spent a long time helping the world find its way through software. It's time I let the world find me here.

Welcome to the new home. Let's build.
